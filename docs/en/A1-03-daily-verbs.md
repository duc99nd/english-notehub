# Daily Verbs

## My Daily Routine

Every day, I follow a simple routine. Here is what I do:

> **I wake up** at six in the morning. The sun is not up yet.
>
> **I eat** breakfast at seven. I usually eat bread and drink coffee.
>
> **I go** to work at eight. My office is not far from home.
>
> **I work** from nine to five. I sit at my desk and use the computer.
>
> **I come** home at six. I am tired but happy.
>
> **I eat** dinner at seven. I cook food or order delivery.
>
> **I watch** TV or read books in the evening.
>
> **I sleep** at ten or eleven. I need good sleep for my health.

## Using "I" with verbs

With **I**, we use the verb directly (no -s at the end):

> **Pattern**: `I [verb]` — I eat, I drink, I work, I sleep.

### Examples

| English                      | Vietnamese                  |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| I eat rice every day.        | Tôi ăn cơm mỗi ngày.        |
| I drink milk in the morning. | Tôi uống sữa vào buổi sáng. |
| I read the news online.      | Tôi đọc tin tức trên mạng.  |
