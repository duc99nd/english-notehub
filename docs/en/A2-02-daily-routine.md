# Daily Routine

## Present Simple for habits

We use the **present simple** to talk about daily routines and habits.

> **Pattern**: `Subject + verb (+ s/es) + time expression`

### Rules

- With **I, you, we, they**: verb stays the same
- With **he, she, it**: add **s** or **es**

| Subject          | Verb  | Example           |
| ---------------- | ----- | ----------------- |
| I, You, We, They | work  | I work every day. |
| He, She, It      | works | She works at 8am. |

## Common time expressions

| English       | Vietnamese    |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| every morning | mỗi buổi sáng |
| every day     | mỗi ngày      |
| usually       | thường thì    |
| always        | luôn luôn     |
| never         | không bao giờ |

## Examples of daily routine

| English                       | Vietnamese                 |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| I wake up at six.             | Tôi thức dậy lúc 6 giờ.    |
| I have breakfast at seven.    | Tôi ăn sáng lúc 7 giờ.     |
| She goes to school by bus.    | Cô ấy đi học bằng xe buýt. |
| They watch TV in the evening. | Họ xem TV vào buổi tối.    |
