# Greetings & Self-introduction

## Hello, my name is...

The most common way to greet someone in English is **"Hello"**. After that, you usually say your name.

> **Pattern**: `Hello, my name is [name].`

### Examples

| English | Vietnamese |
| --- | --- |
| Hello, my name is Anna. | Xin chào, tôi tên là Anna. |
| Hi, I'm Duc. | Chào, mình là Đức. |
| Nice to meet you. | Rất vui được gặp bạn. |

## Formal vs informal

Use **formal** greetings at work and with strangers. Use **informal** greetings with friends.

- **Formal**: `Hello`, `Good morning`, `Nice to meet you`
- **Informal**: `Hi`, `Hey`, `What's up`
