# Travel Blogs & Postcards

## Reading Skills

When you read travel blogs and postcards, focus on:

- Main ideas and key details
- New vocabulary in context
- Descriptive language for places

## Reading 1: Travel Blog Post

### My Amazing Week in Barcelona

Hi everyone! I'm writing this from a small café near the beach. After three days here, I can say that Barcelona is one of the most beautiful cities I've ever visited.

The architecture is absolutely stunning — I've been to the Sagrada Familia twice and I still can't believe how incredible it is. Yesterday, I walked along Las Ramblas and got completely lost in the narrow streets of the Gothic Quarter. I stumbled upon a tiny restaurant where I had the most delicious seafood paella.

The weather has been amazing — sunny and warm every day. I've spent hours on the beach reading and swimming. Tonight, I'm planning to watch the sunset from Park Güell.

If you're thinking about visiting Barcelona, I highly recommend staying at least a week. There's so much to see and do!

---

## Reading 2: Postcard

> **From: Tom**
> **To: Sarah**
>
> Dear Sarah,
>
> Just a quick note from Greece! The islands are even more beautiful than I imagined. The water is crystal clear and the sunsets are unforgettable. Met some great people at the hostel — we're planning to rent a boat tomorrow!
>
> Wish you were here!
> Love, Tom

## Reading Comprehension

1. How long did the blogger stay in Barcelona?
2. What did they have for lunch?
3. What are Sarah's impressions of Greece?

## Practice lab

### Notice the language

Read the situation, then listen to the model once for meaning and once for phrasing.

> **Colleague:** How would you handle **travel blogs & postcards** in this situation?
>
> **You:** Hi everyone! I'm writing this from a small café in Rome.

Notice what the response does before you copy its wording: it gives the listener a clear point, enough context, and a usable next step.

> **Pattern**: clear point + concrete detail or reason + appropriate next step

### Your turn

Summarize the text in two sentences for a colleague or classmate. Identify one claim, one supporting detail, and one phrase that signals the writer's attitude.

### Model response

> Hi everyone! I'm writing this from a small café in Rome. The weather has been amazing and I've been exploring every day. I can't wait to share more photos!

The model is one possible response, not a script you must memorize. Compare its purpose, level of detail, and tone with your version, then revise once.
