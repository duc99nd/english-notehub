# Review Unit 2

## What We Learned

In Unit 2, you learned four important skills for the workplace:

1. **Work & Daily Routines** - Vocabulary for jobs, workplace, and work activities
2. **Modals of Obligation & Advice** - must, have to, should, ought to
3. **Lifestyle Article** - Reading and understanding articles
4. **Job Application Letter** - Writing professional job applications

## Quick Review

### Work Vocabulary

| Category   | Examples                                               |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Jobs       | manager, employee, colleague, freelancer               |
| Workplace  | office, meeting room, desk, break room                 |
| Activities | attend a meeting, meet a deadline, give a presentation |

### Modals Review

| Modal    | Use                 | Example                    |
| -------- | ------------------- | -------------------------- |
| must     | internal obligation | I must finish this today.  |
| have to  | external obligation | I have to wear a uniform.  |
| should   | advice              | You should take a break.   |
| ought to | advice (formal)     | You ought to see a doctor. |

### Negative Forms

| Form          | Meaning       |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| mustn't       | prohibited    |
| don't have to | not necessary |
| shouldn't     | not advisable |

### Job Application Structure

1. Your address + Date
2. Recipient's address
3. Salutation: Dear Mr./Ms. [Name]
4. Opening: State the position
5. Body: Skills and experience
6. Closing: Thank and express interest
7. Sign-off: Yours faithfully

## Practice Activities

### Exercise 1: Choose the correct modal

1. You (must / should) see a doctor — you look pale.
2. I (have to / should) be at the airport by 6 PM.
3. You (mustn't / shouldn't) share passwords with anyone.

### Exercise 2: Complete the job application phrase

1. I am writing to \_\_\_ for the Marketing Manager position.
2. I have five years of \_\_\_ in digital marketing.
3. Thank you for \_\_\_ my application.
