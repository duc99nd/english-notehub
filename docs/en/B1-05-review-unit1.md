# Review Unit 1

## What We Learned

In Unit 1, you learned four important skills for talking about travel:

1. **Travel & Transport Vocabulary** - Words for transport, travel activities, and useful phrases
2. **Past Tenses & Narratives** - Past Simple, Past Continuous, and Past Perfect
3. **Travel Blogs & Reading** - Reading real travel writing
4. **Email to a Friend** - Writing personal, friendly emails

## Quick Review

### Travel Vocabulary

| Category   | Examples                                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Transport  | flight, train, bus, taxi, ferry          |
| Activities | to book, check in, board, depart, arrive |
| At airport | boarding pass, luggage, check-in desk    |

### Past Tenses

| Tense           | Use                                | Example                             |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Past Simple     | Completed actions                  | I **went** to Paris.                |
| Past Continuous | Actions in progress                | I **was sleeping** when you called. |
| Past Perfect    | Actions before another past action | I **had left** before she arrived.  |

### Time Connectors

- when, while, before, after, by the time, as soon as

### Email Structure

1. Greeting: Hi [Name]
2. Opening: How are you? It's been a while...
3. Body: Main message
4. Closing: Love / Best / Take care
5. Signature: Your name
