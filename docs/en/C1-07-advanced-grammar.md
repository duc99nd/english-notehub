# Advanced C1 Grammar

## Inversion for emphasis

Inversion places the verb before the subject to add emphasis. It is common after negative adverbials.

> **Pattern**: `Negative adverbial + auxiliary + subject + verb`

| English                                                               | Vietnamese                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Never have we seen such rapid growth.                                 | Chúng tôi chưa bao giờ thấy tăng trưởng nhanh như vậy.                   |
| Hardly had the meeting begun when the fire alarm rang.                | Cuộc họp vừa bắt đầu thì chuông báo cháy kêu.                            |
| Not only did the merger succeed, but it exceeded expectations.        | Không chỉ thương vụ thành công, mà còn vượt kỳ vọng.                     |
| Under no circumstances should confidential data be shared externally. | Trong bất kỳ trường hợp nào, dữ liệu bí mật không được chia sẻ ra ngoài. |

## Subjunctive mood

The subjunctive expresses wishes, hypotheticals, and strong recommendations.

> **Pattern**: `It is essential/crucial/imperative that + subject + base verb (subjunctive)`

| English                                                 | Vietnamese                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| It is essential that every stakeholder **be** informed. | Điều cần thiết là mọi bên liên quan phải được thông báo.   |
| We recommend that the board **approve** the proposal.   | Chúng tôi khuyến nghị hội đồng phê duyệt đề xuất.          |
| It is vital that we **act** before the deadline.        | Điều quan trọng là chúng ta phải hành động trước hạn chót. |

## Cleft sentences for focus

> **Pattern A**: `What + clause + is/was + [focus]`
> **Pattern B**: `It + be + [focus] + that/who + clause`

| Pattern                                          | Example                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| What concerns us is the timeline.                | Điều khiến chúng tôi lo ngại là tiến độ.          |
| It was the CFO who ultimately approved the deal. | Chính CFO là người cuối cùng phê duyệt thương vụ. |

## Participle clauses (advanced)

> **Pattern**: `Having/V-ed + [clause]`

| English                                            | Vietnamese                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Having reviewed the data, we recommend approval.   | Sau khi xem xét dữ liệu, chúng tôi đề xuất phê duyệt. |
| Considered from all angles, the proposal is sound. | Xét từ mọi góc, đề xuất là hợp lý.                    |

## Cleft vs. inversion

| Device      | Use                                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Inversion   | Emphasize a negative/limiting adverbial    |
| Cleft       | Focus on one specific element              |
| Subjunctive | Express hypothetical/strong recommendation |

## Practice lab

### Notice the language

Read the situation, then listen to the model once for meaning and once for phrasing.

> **Colleague:** How would you handle **advanced c1 grammar** in this situation?
>
> **You:** Had we anticipated the disruption, we would have diversified earlier.

Notice what the response does before you copy its wording: it gives the listener a clear point, enough context, and a usable next step.

> **Pattern**: clear point + concrete detail or reason + appropriate next step

### Your turn

Write four connected sentences for a colleague, client, and senior stakeholder. Use the target structure twice, then revise one sentence to change its tone or emphasis.

### Model response

> Had we anticipated the disruption, we would have diversified earlier. Not only did the merger create synergies, but it also expanded our reach. It is imperative that every stakeholder be informed of the changes.

The model is one possible response, not a script you must memorize. Compare its purpose, level of detail, and tone with your version, then revise once.

### Register switch

Rewrite your response twice: first for a trusted teammate, then for a client or senior stakeholder. Keep the underlying message the same while changing directness, context, and the amount of explanation. Identify one phrase you softened, one detail you made explicit, and one assumption you removed.
