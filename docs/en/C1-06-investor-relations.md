# Investor Relations

## Communicating with the financial community

Investor relations (IR) requires precise, confident communication. Every word must convey both opportunity and discipline.

> **Pattern**: `We project [metric] growth of [X]% over [timeframe]`

### Key IR vocabulary

| Term                         | Definition                 | Example                                  |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **earnings per share (EPS)** | Profit divided by shares   | "EPS grew 12% YoY."                      |
| **fiscal year**              | Company financial year     | "FY2025 closes in March."                |
| **guidance**                 | Forward-looking forecast   | "We reaffirm our full-year guidance."    |
| **consensus**                | Average analyst estimate   | "We beat consensus expectations."        |
| **outperform**               | Exceed market expectations | "Our returns outperformed peers."        |
| **shareholder value**        | Returns to shareholders    | "We are committed to shareholder value." |

## Earnings call language

| Phrase                               | Function         |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| "We are pleased to report..."        | Positive opening |
| "We project / We anticipate..."      | Forward-looking  |
| "We delivered on our commitments..." | Execution proof  |
| "We remain disciplined in..."        | Risk management  |

## Phrases for analyst questions

| Situation           | Phrase                                          |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Reaffirming outlook | "We are reaffirming our full-year guidance."    |
| Addressing concerns | "We hear the concerns and are addressing them." |
| Defending strategy  | "Our strategy remains sound and well-executed." |
| Showing confidence  | "We are confident in our long-term trajectory." |
