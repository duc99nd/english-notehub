# Writing a Job Application Letter

## Structure of a Job Application

A formal job application letter has these sections:

1. **Your address** (top right)
2. **Date** (below your address)
3. **Recipient's address** (left side)
4. **Salutation**: Dear Mr./Ms. [Name]
5. **Opening paragraph**: Introduce yourself and state the position
6. **Body**: Explain your skills and experience
7. **Closing**: Thank them and express interest
8. **Sign-off**: Yours faithfully / Yours sincerely

> **Pattern**: `I am writing to apply for [position]. I have [experience/skills].`

## Useful Phrases

### Opening

- `I am writing to apply for the [position] advertised on [website].`
- `With reference to your advertisement for [position], I would like to apply.`
- `I was excited to see your vacancy for [position].`

### Describing skills

- `I have [X years] of experience in [field].`
- `I am skilled in [skill].`
- `I am proficient in [software/tool].`
- `I have excellent communication skills.`

### Closing

- `I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application.`
- `I look forward to hearing from you.`
- `Thank you for considering my application.`

## Example Application Letter

> **John Smith**
> 123 Main Street, London, SW1 4AA
> john.smith@email.com
>
> 20 August 2026
>
> **HR Manager**
> ABC Company Ltd.
> 456 Business Road
> London, EC1 2AB
>
> Dear Mr. Johnson,
>
> I am writing to apply for the Marketing Manager position advertised on your website. With five years of experience in digital marketing, I believe I am well-suited for this role.
>
> In my current position at XYZ Company, I have managed campaigns that increased sales by 25%. I am skilled in social media marketing, content creation, and data analysis. I have excellent communication skills and enjoy working in a team.
>
> I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application further. I can be reached at john.smith@email.com or 07700 900123.
>
> Thank you for considering my application.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> John Smith
