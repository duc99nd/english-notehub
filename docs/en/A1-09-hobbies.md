# Hobbies & Free Time

## My Free Time

Everyone has hobbies. Here are some common hobbies:

> **Reading**: I like to read books. I read every night before I sleep.
>
> **Music**: I enjoy listening to music. Sometimes I play the guitar.
>
> **Sports**: I play soccer on weekends. It keeps me healthy.
>
> **Cooking**: I like cooking Vietnamese food. My favorite dish is pho.
>
> **Movies**: I watch movies on Friday nights. I like action films.
>
> **Games**: I play video games with my friends online.

## Expressing Preferences

Use **"I like"**, **"I love"**, or **"I enjoy"**:

> **Pattern**: `I like to [verb].`
>
> **Pattern**: `I enjoy [verb + ing].`

### Examples

| English                     | Vietnamese                  |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| I like to read books.       | Tôi thích đọc sách.         |
| I enjoy listening to music. | Tôi thích nghe nhạc.        |
| I love playing soccer.      | Tôi rất thích chơi bóng đá. |
| She likes cooking.          | Cô ấy thích nấu ăn.         |

## Writing about your hobbies

Try to write about your hobbies:

> `In my free time, I like to [hobby].`
>
> `I also enjoy [another hobby].`
>
> `My favorite hobby is [hobby] because [reason].`

### Example

`In my free time, I like to read books. I also enjoy listening to music. My favorite hobby is reading because books are interesting.`
