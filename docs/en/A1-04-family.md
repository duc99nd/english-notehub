# Family Members

## My Family

I have a big family. Let me tell you about them:

> **My mother** is kind and smart. She cooks food for us every day.
>
> **My father** works hard. He comes home late in the evening.
>
> **My sister** is a student. She studies English at university.
>
> **My brother** is a teacher. He teaches math at a high school.

## How to talk about your family

Use **"I have"** to talk about family members:

> **Pattern**: `I have [one/two] [sister/sisters].`

### Examples

| English              | Vietnamese            |
| -------------------- | --------------------- |
| I have one sister.   | Tôi có một chị gái.   |
| I have two brothers. | Tôi có hai anh trai.  |
| I have no sisters.   | Tôi không có chị gái. |

## Using "This is"

Use **"This is"** to introduce family members:

> **Pattern**: `This is my [family member].`

### Examples

| English                               | Vietnamese                               |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| This is my mother. Her name is Lan.   | Đây là mẹ tôi. Tên cô ấy là Lan.         |
| This is my brother. His name is Tuan. | Đây là anh trai tôi. Tên anh ấy là Tuấn. |
| This is my grandmother. She is 70.    | Đây là bà tôi. Bà 70 tuổi.               |

## Writing exercise

Try to write three sentences about your family:

1. `This is my ___. Her/His name is ___.`
2. `I have ___ sisters and ___ brothers.`
3. `My ___ is ___ years old.`
