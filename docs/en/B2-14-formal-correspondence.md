# Formal Correspondence

## Writing professional letters and emails

Formal English follows strict conventions for openings, closings, and tone. Use these templates for any professional communication.

### Standard structure

| Part     | Example                              |
| -------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Opening  | Dear Sir or Madam, / Dear Mr. Smith, |
| Purpose  | I am writing to inquire about...     |
| Detail   | I would be grateful if you could...  |
| Closing  | I look forward to hearing from you.  |
| Sign-off | Yours sincerely, / Yours faithfully, |

### Formal openings

- Dear Sir or Madam, (when name unknown)
- Dear Mr. / Ms. [Last Name], (when name known)
- To Whom It May Concern, (general)

### Formal closings

- Yours sincerely, (when name known)
- Yours faithfully, (when name unknown)
- Kind regards, (slightly less formal)

## Useful phrases

| English                               | Vietnamese                       |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| I am writing to inquire about...      | Tôi viết để hỏi về...            |
| I would be grateful if you could...   | Tôi sẽ biết ơn nếu bạn có thể... |
| I look forward to hearing from you.   | Tôi mong nhận được hồi âm.       |
| At your earliest convenience          | Khi nào thuận tiện               |
| Please do not hesitate to contact me. | Đừng ngần ngại liên hệ với tôi.  |

## Sample email

> Dear Ms. Johnson,
>
> I am writing to inquire about the position advertised on your website. I would be grateful if you could provide further information about the role.
>
> I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> [Your Name]
