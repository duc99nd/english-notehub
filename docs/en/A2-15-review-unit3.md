# Review Unit 3

## What we learned

In Unit 3, you built everyday conversation skills:

1. **Small talk** — Opening friendly conversations
2. **Opinions** — Sharing your ideas politely
3. **Experiences** — Talking about your life using present perfect
4. **Short reading** — Understanding personal stories

## Quick review

### Small talk starters

| English | Vietnamese |
| --- | --- |
| Lovely weather, isn't it? | Thời tiết đẹp nhỉ? |
| How was your weekend? | Cuối tuần của bạn thế nào? |
| Do you come here often? | Bạn hay đến đây không? |

### Opinion phrases

| English | Vietnamese |
| --- | --- |
| I think... | Tôi nghĩ... |
| I believe... | Tôi tin rằng... |
| In my opinion... | Theo ý kiến của tôi... |
| I agree / I disagree | Tôi đồng ý / không đồng ý |

### Present perfect pattern

| Form | Example |
| --- | --- |
| have/has + V3 | I have visited Japan. |
| ever | Have you ever tried sushi? |
| never | I have never seen snow. |
| for + time | I have lived here for 5 years. |
| since + time | I have worked here since 2018. |

### Reading strategy

| Strategy | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Look for known words | Builds confidence |
| Find time markers | Anchors events |
| Identify verbs | Understands actions |
