# Weather

## My Day in the Park

Yesterday was a beautiful day. Let me tell you about it:

> **It was sunny** in the morning. The sky was blue and there were no clouds. The sun was bright and warm.
>
> **It became cloudy** in the afternoon. Some white clouds appeared in the sky. It was still warm, not cold.
>
> **It rained** in the evening. I was at home, so I was dry. The rain was not heavy, just light rain.
>
> **Today is windy**. The wind is strong. I wear a jacket when I go outside.

## Seasons

| English       | Vietnamese | Weather              |
| ------------- | ---------- | -------------------- |
| spring        | mùa xuân   | warm, flowers bloom  |
| summer        | mùa hè     | hot, sunny           |
| autumn / fall | mùa thu    | cool, leaves change  |
| winter        | mùa đông   | cold, sometimes snow |

## How to talk about weather

> **Pattern**: `It is [weather] today.`
>
> **Pattern**: `It was [weather] yesterday.`

### Examples

| English                  | Vietnamese                      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| It is sunny today.       | Hôm nay trời nắng.              |
| It is cold in winter.    | Mùa đông trời lạnh.             |
| It rained yesterday.     | Hôm qua trời mưa.               |
