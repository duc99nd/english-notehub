# Weather

## Why weather words are universal

Weather is the #1 small-talk topic in English. You can start a conversation with a stranger, your boss, your doctor — anyone — with "Lovely day, isn't it?" or "Terrible weather today". Knowing ten weather words + five small-talk phrases unlocks unlimited conversation starters.

## Weather adjectives

| English | Vietnamese    | Use with "it is"   |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| sunny   | nắng          | It is sunny today. |
| cloudy  | có mây / u ám | It is cloudy.      |
| rainy   | mưa (tính từ) | It is rainy today. |
| windy   | có gió        | It is windy.       |
| hot     | nóng          | It is hot.         |
| cold    | lạnh          | It is cold.        |
| warm    | ấm            | It is warm.        |
| cool    | mát / se lạnh | It is cool.        |
| snowy   | có tuyết      | It is snowy.       |
| humid   | ẩm ướt        | It is humid.       |
| foggy   | có sương mù   | It is foggy.       |
| stormy  | có bão        | It is stormy.      |

> **Pattern**: All weather adjectives go with **"it is"**, NOT "the weather is" or "today is". "It is **sunny**" — never "It is **sun**" or "It is **a sun**."

## Weather nouns (the rain, the wind, the sun)

When you want to talk about the **thing** (not just the adjective), use the noun with "the":

| Noun            | Vietnamese | Example                  |
| --------------- | ---------- | ------------------------ |
| the sun         | mặt trời   | The sun is bright today. |
| the rain        | mưa        | The rain is heavy.       |
| the wind        | gió        | The wind is strong.      |
| the snow        | tuyết      | The snow is white.       |
| the cloud       | mây        | The clouds are dark.     |
| the sky         | bầu trời   | The sky is blue.         |
| the temperature | nhiệt độ   | The temperature is 30°C. |
| the weather     | thời tiết  | The weather is nice.     |

> **Pattern**: "The **noun** is + adjective". "The rain is heavy." "The wind is strong." This pattern is great for adding details after "It is \_\_\_".

## Weather verbs

Three useful verbs go with weather.

> **Pattern A — rain / snow (verb, no "it is")**: `It rains a lot in summer.`
>
> **Pattern B — get + adjective (changing weather)**: `It gets cold in December.`
>
> **Pattern C — present progressive (right now)**: `It is raining right now.`

| Verb form   | Example                              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------ |
| rain (verb) | It rains a lot in April. (habit)     |
| snow (verb) | It snows in winter. (sometimes true) |
| is raining  | It is raining right now. (right now) |
| is snowing  | It is snowing! (right now)           |
| get / gets  | It gets hot in May. (changing)       |

> **Tip**: Use **is + -ing** ("is raining", "is snowing") for **right now**. Use **simple form** ("rains", "snows") for **usually / often**.

## Seasons

| English       | Vietnamese | Weather              |
| ------------- | ---------- | -------------------- |
| spring        | mùa xuân   | warm, flowers bloom  |
| summer        | mùa hè     | hot, sunny, humid    |
| autumn / fall | mùa thu    | cool, leaves change  |
| winter        | mùa đông   | cold, sometimes snow |

> **Note**: "Autumn" and "fall" mean the same thing. "Fall" is more common in the US; "autumn" is more common in the UK and in writing.

### Talking about seasons

> **Pattern**: `In [season], it is [weather].`

| English                                  | Vietnamese                  |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| In spring, it is warm and flowers bloom. | Mùa xuân trời ấm và hoa nở. |
| In summer, it is hot and humid.          | Mùa hè trời nóng và ẩm.     |
| In autumn, it is cool.                   | Mùa thu trời mát.           |
| In winter, it is cold.                   | Mùa đông trời lạnh.         |

## Small talk — five weather phrases

> **Pattern A — Open a conversation**: `Lovely day, isn't it?`
>
> **Pattern B — Comment**: `It's [weather] today, isn't it?`
>
> **Pattern C — Ask the future**: `What's the weather like tomorrow?`
>
> **Pattern D — Complaining**: `Terrible weather!`
>
> **Pattern E — Agreeing**: `Yes, but I heard it will be sunny tomorrow.`

| Phrase                        | Tone               |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Lovely day, isn't it?         | friendly           |
| Beautiful weather today!      | friendly           |
| It's so hot today!            | casual             |
| It's freezing!                | casual (very cold) |
| What a terrible day.          | complaining        |
| Perfect weather for a walk.   | friendly           |
| I wish it would stop raining. | casual             |

> **Tip**: Weather is a "safe topic" — it can't offend anyone. Use these phrases with strangers, in elevators, at the bus stop.

## Listen to a real weather conversation

> **Anna**: Lovely day, isn't it?
>
> **Duc**: Yes, it's beautiful. The sun is bright but not too hot.
>
> **Anna**: I heard it will be cloudy tomorrow.
>
> **Duc**: Really? I need to check. I have a picnic planned on Sunday.
>
> **Anna**: If it rains, you can move it to next weekend.
>
> **Duc**: True. By the way, do you like winter?
>
> **Anna**: Not really. It's too cold and dark. I prefer spring. The weather is perfect — warm but not hot.
>
> **Duc**: Same here. In Vietnam, winter is short but cold in the North. I live in Hanoi so I have to wear a jacket.
>
> **Anna**: I live in Da Nang so it's warm all year. We don't really have winter.
>
> **Duc**: That's nice. What about summer?
>
> **Anna**: Summer is very hot. The temperature is 35-38°C. It's humid too.
>
> **Duc**: Yeah, Hanoi summer is also hot. June to August is the worst.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ "Today is **sun**." — sun is a noun, not adjective. ✅ "Today is **sunny**."
- ❌ "The weather is **rain**." — same. ✅ "It is **rainy** today."
- ❌ "It is **a cold** today." — cold is adjective, no "a". ✅ "It is **cold** today."
- ❌ "I like **winter weathers**." — weather is uncountable. ✅ "I like **winter weather**."
- ❌ "It **rain** yesterday." — verb agreement. ✅ "It **rained** yesterday." (past — you'll learn in A2)

## Culture note: weather around the world

**Vietnam**: Tropical country with two main seasons in the South (rainy / dry) and four in the North (spring, summer, autumn, winter). Winters in Hanoi can drop to 10°C; summers hit 38°C. Da Nang and HCMC stay warm year-round.

**UK**: Famous for rain — "It's always raining in England" is a stereotype (and partly true). Winters are short days (4 PM sunset). Summers are mild, 20-25°C.

**US**: Big country, every climate. New York has hot summers and cold winters. California is mostly warm and dry. Florida is humid and warm year-round.

**"How's the weather?"** is a standard greeting in the US and UK, like "how are you". Vietnamese people don't usually open with weather — but in English, it's normal.
