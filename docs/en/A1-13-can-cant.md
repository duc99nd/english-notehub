# Can and Can't

## What are can and can't?

We use **can** and **can't** to talk about abilities. Can means you are able to do something. Can't means you are not able to do something.

## Positive form (can)

> **Pattern**: `Subject + can + verb`

|| English | Vietnamese |
|| --- | --- |
|| I can swim. | Tôi biết bơi. |
|| She can speak English. | Cô ấy có thể nói tiếng Anh. |
|| He can play the piano. | Anh ấy biết chơi piano. |
|| They can cook well. | Họ có thể nấu ăn ngon. |
|| We can run fast. | Chúng tôi có thể chạy nhanh. |

## Negative form (can't)

> **Pattern**: `Subject + can't + verb`

|| English | Vietnamese |
|| --- | --- |
|| I can't sing. | Tôi không biết hát. |
|| She can't drive a car. | Cô ấy không biết lái xe. |
|| He can't speak Chinese. | Anh ấy không thể nói tiếng Trung. |
|| They can't come today. | Họ không thể đến hôm nay. |

## Questions (Can...?)

> **Pattern**: `Can + subject + verb?`

|| English | Vietnamese |
|| --- | --- |
|| Can you swim? | Bạn có biết bơi không? |
|| Can she speak English? | Cô ấy có thể nói tiếng Anh không? |
|| Can he cook? | Anh ấy có thể nấu ăn không? |
|| Can they come? | Họ có thể đến không? |

## Short answers

|| Question | Short Answer (Yes) | Short Answer (No) |
|| --- | --- | --- |
|| Can you swim? | Yes, I can. | No, I can't. |
|| Can she cook? | Yes, she can. | No, she can't. |
|| Can he drive? | Yes, he can. | No, he can't. |
|| Can they come? | Yes, they can. | No, they can't. |
