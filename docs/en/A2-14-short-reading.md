# Short Reading

## Read two short stories

### Story 1: Maria

> Maria is 28 years old. She **has traveled** to twelve countries. She **has tried** food from many places. She **has never** eaten horse meat, but she **has** tried snails in France. Maria **has lived** in London for five years.

### Story 2: Tom

> Tom **has worked** at the same company since 2018. He **has finished** three big projects this year. He **has just** completed a new course. Tom **has never** changed jobs.

## Key vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| traveled | visited places |
| tried | attempted / tested |
| finished | completed |
| just | recently |
| completed | finished |

## Reading comprehension

Answer the questions:

1. How many countries has Maria visited?
2. When did Tom start working at his company?
3. Has Tom ever changed jobs?
4. What has Maria never eaten?

## Quick answers

1. Twelve countries.
2. In 2018.
3. No, he has never changed jobs.
4. Horse meat.

## Reading strategy

| Strategy | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Look for known words | "worked", "countries", "London" |
| Find time markers | "for five years", "since 2018" |
| Identify verbs | "has traveled", "has tried" |
