# Travel & Transport

## Essential Travel Vocabulary

When you travel, you need to know words for different types of transport and travel activities.

> **Pattern**: `I'm going to [destination] by [transport].`

## Types of Transport

|                | English       | Vietnamese |
| -------------- | ------------- | ---------- |
| flight         | chuyến bay    |
| train          | tàu hỏa       |
| bus            | xe buýt       |
| taxi / cab     | taxi          |
| car            | ô tô          |
| bicycle / bike | xe đạp        |
| ferry          | phà           |
| cruise         | du thuyền     |
| subway / metro | tàu điện ngầm |

## Travel Activities

|                      | English              | Vietnamese |
| -------------------- | -------------------- | ---------- |
| to book / to reserve | đặt (vé, phòng)      |
| to check in          | làm thủ tục check-in |
| to board             | lên máy bay/tàu      |
| to take off          | cất cánh             |
| to land              | hạ cánh              |
| to depart            | khởi hành            |
| to arrive            | đến nơi              |
| to delay             | bị trễ               |
| to cancel            | bị hủy               |

## Useful Travel Phrases

> "I'm planning a trip to [city]."
> "I need to renew my passport."
> "Can you tell me how to get to the train station?"
> "What time does the next bus depart?"
> "Is there a direct flight?"
> "I'd like to book a window seat."

## At the Airport / Train Station

- `check-in desk` — quầy làm thủ tục
- `departure lounge` — phòng chờ khởi hành
- `boarding pass` — thẻ lên máy bay
- `luggage / baggage` — hành lý
- `to miss a flight` — lỡ chuyến bay
- `to catch a train` — kịp tàu

## Learning outcome

By the end of this lesson, you can complete a four-turn exchange about travel & transport, choosing expressions that fit the relationship and immediate goal.

## Scenario practice

### Situation

You are arranging a short business trip with a colleague. Compare flight and train options, then ask how to reach the client office.

> **Training note:** This situation and its details are fictional. They are designed for language practice, not presented as a real event, policy, study, or legal interpretation.

### Controlled practice

Choose three expressions from **Travel & Transport**. Label each one as an opener, key detail, clarification, or next step. Remove any expression that does not fit the relationship in the situation.

### Guided task

Write a four-to-six-turn exchange for this situation. Use at least three target expressions, respond to the other speaker's information, and end with a question, confirmation, or agreed action.

### Model response

> **Partner:** We need to respond to the situation above. What would you say first?
>
> **You:** I'm planning a trip to Barcelona next month.
>
> **Partner:** What detail should we confirm before we act?
>
> **You:** I usually take a flight because it's faster. Can you tell me how to get to the train station?

### Why the model works

The model shows one defensible route through the task; it is not a script to memorize. Compare its task completion, language accuracy, audience fit, and clarity with your response, then revise once.

### Transfer task

Revise the plan after the preferred flight is cancelled. Keep the target language, but change the details and register needed for the new context.
