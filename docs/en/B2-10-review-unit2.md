In Unit 2, you built on your B1 foundation with five advanced skills:

1. **Meeting Facilitation** — leading meetings with confidence

3. **Business Article Reading** — analyzing market reports

5. **Data Storytelling** — presenting insights effectively

Plus: **Advanced Mixed Conditionals** for hypothetical situations.

## Quick review

### Meeting facilitation phrases

| Phrase          | Use               |
| --------------- | ----------------- |
| kick off        | Start the meeting |
| park that topic | Defer to later    |
| tiebreaker      | Resolve deadlock  |
| draw to a close | End the meeting   |

### Persuasive grammar patterns

- **Cleft**: What matters is the outcome.
- **Inversion**: Not only does it reduce costs, but it also improves quality.
- **Double comparative**: The more we test, the better the result becomes.

### Conditionals

| Type  | Structure                      | Use                    |
| ----- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| Third | If + had + pp, would have + pp | Past hypothetical      |
| Mixed | past + present                 | Connecting time frames |

### Notice the language

Read the situation, then listen to the model once for meaning and once for phrasing.

> **Colleague:** How would you handle **review unit 2** in this situation?
>
> **You:** Let's review Unit 2 of B2.

Notice what the response does before you copy its wording: it gives the listener a clear point, enough context, and a usable next step.

> **Pattern**: clear point + concrete detail or reason + appropriate next step

### Your turn

The model is one possible response, not a script you must memorize. Compare its purpose, level of detail, and tone with your version, then revise once.

### Register switch

Keep the underlying message the same while changing directness, context, and the amount of explanation. Identify one phrase you softened, one detail you made explicit, and one assumption you removed.
