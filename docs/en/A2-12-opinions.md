# Opinions

## Expressing opinions

You can share your thoughts and beliefs using these phrases.

> **Pattern**: `I think/believe/feel that [opinion]`

## Common opinion phrases

| English          | Vietnamese             |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| I think...       | Tôi nghĩ...            |
| I believe...     | Tôi tin rằng...        |
| I feel...        | Tôi cảm thấy...        |
| In my opinion... | Theo ý kiến của tôi... |

## Examples

| English                                  | Vietnamese                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| I think this is a great movie.           | Tôi nghĩ đây là một bộ phim hay.       |
| I believe learning English is important. | Tôi tin rằng học tiếng Anh quan trọng. |
| I feel tired today.                      | Tôi cảm thấy mệt hôm nay.              |
| In my opinion, we should wait.           | Theo ý kiến tôi, chúng ta nên đợi.     |

## Agreeing and disagreeing

| English                  | Vietnamese                    |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| I agree with you.        | Tôi đồng ý với bạn.           |
| I disagree with that.    | Tôi không đồng ý với điều đó. |
| That's a good point.     | Đó là một điểm tốt.           |
| I'm not sure about that. | Tôi không chắc về điều đó.    |

## Structure

Opinion phrases go at the beginning or end of a sentence.

- `I think [something] is [adjective].`
- `[Something] is [adjective], I think.`

## Practice lab

### Notice the language

Read the situation, then listen to the model once for meaning and once for phrasing.

> **Colleague:** How would you handle **opinions** in this situation?
>
> **You:** I think this is a great movie.

Notice what the response does before you copy its wording: it gives the listener a clear point, enough context, and a usable next step.

> **Pattern**: clear point + concrete detail or reason + appropriate next step

### Your turn

Write four connected sentences for a colleague or classmate. Use the target structure twice, then revise one sentence to change its tone or emphasis.

### Model response

> I think this is a great movie. In my opinion, we should leave early. I believe learning English is important.

The model is one possible response, not a script you must memorize. Compare its purpose, level of detail, and tone with your version, then revise once.
