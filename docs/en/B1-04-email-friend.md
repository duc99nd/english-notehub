# Writing an Email to a Friend

## Email Structure

A friendly email to a friend has these parts:

1. **Greeting**: Hi / Hey + name
2. **Opening**: How are you? It's been a while...
3. **Body**: Main message / news
4. **Closing**: Hope to hear from you / Love / Best
5. **Signature**: Your name

> **Pattern**: `Hi [Name], [Greeting]. [Body]. [Closing], [Name].`

## Useful Phrases

### Starting the email

- `Hi [Name], it's been a while!`
- `Hey! How are you doing?`
- `I hope you're well.`

### Sharing news

- `I'm writing to tell you about...`
- `You'll never guess what happened!`
- `I wanted to share some exciting news...`

### Making plans

- `Are you free next weekend?`
- `Let me know when you're available.`
- `It would be great to catch up soon.`

### Closing

- `Hope to hear from you soon!`
- `Take care!`
- `Love, [Name]`
- `Best, [Name]`

## Example Email

> **To:** emma.johnson@email.com
> **Subject:** My Trip to Tokyo!
>
> Hi Emma,
>
> It's been a while since we last spoke! I hope you're doing well.
>
> I'm writing to tell you about my trip to Tokyo last month. It was absolutely amazing! The city is so fascinating — I spent hours walking around Shibuya and took so many photos.
>
> The food was incredible. I tried sushi at a tiny restaurant in a narrow alley, and it was the best I've ever had. I also visited the Tokyo Tower and watched the city lights at night.
>
> When are you free? We should catch up and I'll show you all my photos!
>
> Love,
> Sarah

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Don't use formal phrases like "Dear Sir" with friends
- Don't write in all capitals — it looks like SHOUTING
- Keep it natural and conversational
