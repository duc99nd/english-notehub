# Review Unit 1

## What did you learn?

In Unit 1, you learned four important lessons:

1. **Greetings** — How to say hello and introduce yourself
2. **Numbers** — How to count and say phone numbers
3. **Daily Verbs** — Common verbs for everyday activities
4. **Family** — Words for family members

## Quick review

### Greetings

| English              | Vietnamese              |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Hello, my name is... | Xin chào, tôi tên là... |
| Nice to meet you.    | Rất vui được gặp bạn.   |
| Good morning.        | Chào buổi sáng.         |

## Key patterns

> **I have**: `I have one sister.` (Tôi có một chị gái.)
>
> **This is**: `This is my mother.` (Đây là mẹ tôi.)
>
> **My phone number is**: `My phone number is 091 567 890.`

## Practice exercises

### Exercise 1: Fill in the blank

1. `Hello, my ___ is Mai.`
2. `I ___ up at six in the morning.`
3. `I have two ___ and one sister.`

- wake up — thức dậy
- five — năm
