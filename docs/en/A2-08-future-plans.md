# Future Plans

## Two ways to talk about the future

We have two main ways to talk about future plans:

1. **Going to** - Planned decisions
2. **Will** - Spontaneous decisions or predictions

## Going to

Use **going to** for plans you have already made.

> **Pattern**: `Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb`

| Subject | Example                          | Vietnamese                   |
| ------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| I       | I am going to study English.     | Tôi sẽ học tiếng Anh.        |
| He      | He is going to travel to Hanoi.  | Anh ấy sẽ đi du lịch Hà Nội. |
| They    | They are going to buy a new car. | Họ sẽ mua một chiếc xe mới.  |

## Will

Use **will** for:

- Spontaneous decisions (deciding now)
- Predictions without evidence

> **Pattern**: `Subject + will + verb`

| Subject | Example                   | Vietnamese                         |
| ------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| I       | I will help you.          | Tôi sẽ giúp bạn.                   |
| She     | She will call you later.  | Cô ấy sẽ gọi cho bạn sau.          |
| We      | We will see what happens. | Chúng ta sẽ xem điều gì sẽ xảy ra. |

## Going to vs Will

| Going to                          | Will                        |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Planned decision                  | Spontaneous decision        |
| I am going to study tonight.      | I will answer this call.    |
| She is going to visit her mother. | Wait, I will open the door. |
