# Review Unit 2

## What we learned

In Unit 2, you learned four important topics:

1. **Email Basics** - Key vocabulary and phrases for writing emails
2. **Phone Call** - Making and receiving phone calls
3. **Future Plans** - Using going to and will
4. **Should and Must** - Modal verbs for advice and obligation

## Quick review

### Email Basics

- **Greeting**: Dear Sir/Madam, Dear Mr. [Name]
- **Useful phrases**: I am writing to..., Thank you for your email
- **Closing**: Best regards, Yours sincerely

### Phone Call

- **Answer**: Hello, this is [name] speaking
- **Request**: Can I speak to [name]?
- **Message**: Could you take a message?

### Future Plans

- **Going to**: Planned decisions
- **Will**: Spontaneous decisions or predictions

### Should and Must

- **Should**: Advice (softer)
- **Must**: Strong obligation (harder)
