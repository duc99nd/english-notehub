# Pronunciation Guide for IT Terms

Many Vietnamese developers read technical words daily but mispronounce them in meetings. This guide covers the most commonly mispronounced IT terms with phonetic breakdowns using IPA notation.

---

## 1. Database & Infrastructure Words

These are words you say in every standup and architecture discussion.

| Word           | ❌ Common Mistake         | 🟢 Correct (IPA)     | Phonetic Hint                          |
| :------------- | :------------------------ | :------------------- | :------------------------------------- |
| **cache**      | /kash-ee/ or /catch/      | /kæʃ/                | Rhymes with "cash"                     |
| **SQL**        | /sequel/ or /es-queue-el/ | Both are correct!    | "sequel" or "S-Q-L"                    |
| **nginx**      | /en-jinx/                 | /ˌɛndʒɪnˈɛks/        | "engine-X"                             |
| **Ubuntu**     | /yoo-BUN-too/             | /ʊˈbʊntuː/           | "oo-BOON-too" (stress on 2nd syllable) |
| **MySQL**      | /my-sequel/               | /maɪˌɛskjuːˈɛl/      | "my-S-Q-L" (official)                  |
| **PostgreSQL** | /post-gres-quel/          | /ˈpoʊstɡrɛsˌkjuːˈɛl/ | "post-GRES-Q-L"                        |
| **Redis**      | /REE-dis/                 | /ˈrɛdɪs/             | "RED-iss" (like "red" + "is")          |
| **Kubernetes** | /koo-ber-nets/            | /kuːbərˈnɛtiːz/      | "koo-ber-NET-eez"                      |
| **daemon**     | /DAY-mon/                 | /ˈdiːmən/            | "DEE-mun" (not "day-mon")              |
| **schema**     | /SHEE-ma/                 | /ˈskiːmə/            | "SKEE-muh"                             |

## 5. Tricky Pairs (Words That Sound Similar)

Be careful not to confuse these pairs in meetings:

1.  **"cache" /kæʃ/ vs. "catch" /kætʃ/:** "Let me clear the **cache**" ≠ "Let me **catch** the bug."
2.  **"route" /ruːt/ vs. "root" /ruːt/:** In US English, both sound the same. In UK English, "route" is /raʊt/.
3.  **"merge" /mɜːrdʒ/ vs. "much" /mʌtʃ/:** "Please **merge** the PR" — not "Please **much** the PR."
4.  **"queue" /kjuː/ vs. "query" /ˈkwɪri/:** "Add a task to the **queue**" vs. "Run a database **query**."
5.  **"launch" /lɔːntʃ/ vs. "lunch" /lʌntʃ/:** "We will **launch** the app" — not "We will **lunch** the app."
6.  **"log" /lɒɡ/ vs. "lock" /lɒk/:** "Check the server **logs**" vs. "The database is **locked**."
7.  **"height" /haɪt/ vs. "width" /wɪdθ/:** Both are CSS properties. Note: "height" has a silent "gh".
8.  **"pull" /pʊl/ vs. "pool" /puːl/:** "Create a **pull** request" vs. "Configure the connection **pool**."
9.  **"live" /laɪv/ (adj) vs. "live" /lɪv/ (verb):** "The site is **live** /laɪv/" vs. "I **live** /lɪv/ in Hanoi."
10. **"access" /ˈæksɛs/ vs. "assess" /əˈsɛs/:** "Grant **access** to the server" vs. "**Assess** the risk."

## 6. Stress Patterns for Multi-Syllable Words

In English, stressing the wrong syllable can make a word completely unintelligible. Here are common IT words with their correct stress marked in **CAPS**:

1.  **de-VEL-op-er** (not DEV-el-oper)
2.  **ap-pli-CA-tion** (not ap-PLI-cation)
3.  **au-then-ti-CA-tion** (not au-THEN-tication)
4.  **con-fig-u-RA-tion** (not CON-figuration)
5.  **do-cu-men-TA-tion** (not DOC-umentation)
6.  **im-ple-men-TA-tion** (not IM-plementation)
7.  **in-fra-STRUC-ture** (not infra-struc-TURE)
8.  **re-POS-i-to-ry** (not REP-ository)
9.  **ar-chi-TEC-ture** (not ARCH-itecture)
10. **vul-ner-a-BIL-i-ty** (not vul-NER-ability)
