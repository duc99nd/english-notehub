# Review Unit 3

## Unit 3 Summary

Unit 3 completes your C2 journey, integrating advanced research communication, formal debate, scholarly publishing, and creative-professional writing. You now possess the complete range of C2 competencies.

## Key Concepts Recap

### 1. Research Presentation Vocabulary (C2-11)

Research presentations, particularly doctoral defenses, require balancing confidence with epistemic humility. Methodological vocabulary and hedging strategies maintain scholarly credibility.

| Concept                           | Application                                    |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Triangulation                     | Using multiple methods to verify findings      |
| Saturation                        | No new themes emerging in qualitative research |
| "Notwithstanding the limitations" | Acknowledge while defending                    |
| Effect size reporting             | Beyond statistical significance                |

### 2. Formal Debate Grammar (C2-12)

Debate deploys specific grammatical structures for rhetorical impact:

**Key structures:**

- **Cleft sentences**: `It is not X but Y that matters.`
- **Passive constructions**: Impersonal attribution of sources
- **Emphatic "do"**: `The data do show...`
- **Inversion**: `Never has a policy...`

### 3. Writing for Publication (C2-13)

Scholarly publishing follows established conventions:

**Reviewer language:**

```
"Would benefit from" = major revision needed
"Methodology is sound" = at least the methods are okay
"Seems premature" = reject and resubmit later
```

### 4. Professional Poetry (C2-14)

Professional poetry serves occasions, not personal expression:

**Structural pattern:**

1. Acknowledge
2. Invoke
3. Celebrate
4. Commit

## Scenario practice

### Situation

Complete a fictional expert-communication capstone: defend research, debate a motion, answer peer review, and craft a memorable closing for a public event.

> **Training note:** This situation and its details are fictional. They are designed for language practice, not presented as a real event, policy, study, or legal interpretation.

### Controlled practice

Select language from at least three lessons in this unit. For each choice, note the job it performs: describe, connect, qualify, request, or conclude.

### Guided task

Write 75–230 words to complete the integrated task. Use material from at least three lessons, then annotate one revision that improved accuracy, tone, or clarity.

### Model response

> **Integrated response:** Unit 3 completes your C2 journey, integrating research, debate, publishing, and creative professional skills. You now possess the full range of C2 communication competencies. This review consolidates your mastery across all three units.
>
> **Revision note:** I distinguish what the language states from what a reader may plausibly infer. Other well-supported responses are possible.

### Why the model works

The model shows one defensible route through the task; it is not a script to memorize. Compare its task completion, language accuracy, audience fit, and clarity with your response, then revise once.
