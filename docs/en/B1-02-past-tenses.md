# Past Tenses & Narratives

## Three Past Tenses

When telling stories, we use three past tenses together:

- **Past Simple**: completed actions
- **Past Continuous**: actions in progress
- **Past Perfect**: actions before another past action

> **Pattern**: `Subject + was/were + verb-ing` (Past Continuous)

## Past Simple

Use for completed actions in the past.

|           | Base | Past   | Example                       |
| --------- | ---- | ------ | ----------------------------- |
| regular   | work | worked | I worked late yesterday.      |
| irregular | go   | went   | We went to Paris last summer. |
| irregular | eat  | ate    | She ate at a restaurant.      |

## Past Continuous

Use for actions in progress at a specific time in the past.

> **Pattern**: `Subject + was/were + verb-ing`

|                | Subject | Verb                           | Example |
| -------------- | ------- | ------------------------------ | ------- |
| I, He, She, It | was     | I was sleeping at midnight.    |
| You, We, They  | were    | They were waiting for the bus. |

**With Past Simple** (past continuous + past simple):

> "I **was walking** down the street when it **started** raining."
> "She **was reading** when he **called**."

## Past Perfect

Use for actions that happened BEFORE another past action.

> **Pattern**: `Subject + had + past participle`

| Subject | Verb                  | Example                              |
| ------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| All     | had + past participle | I had already left when she arrived. |

> "We **had finished** dinner before the movie **started**."
> "She **had never flown** before that trip."

## Time Connectors

| Connector   | Meaning   |
| ----------- | --------- |
| when        | khi       |
| while       | trong khi |
| before      | trước khi |
| after       | sau khi   |
| by the time | vào lúc   |
| as soon as  | ngay khi  |

## Story Practice

> "Yesterday, I **was walking** to work when I **saw** an old friend. We **hadn't seen** each other for years, so we **went** to a café and **talked** for hours."
