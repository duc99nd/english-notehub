# PRESENTATIONS & PRODUCT DEMOS (WEB APPS)

## Essential phrases for greeting clients, asking questions, and speaking while interacting with web apps

> **How to use this document**
>
> - **Highlighted Sections 1 & 2** are placed at the top for immediate access when you need to demo a web app and talk at the same time.
> - Use these templates to reduce hesitation pauses ("uhm", "ah") during live demonstrations.
> - Focus on short, actionable phrases that match your mouse actions.

---

# 1. LIVE WEB DEMO: SPEAKING WHILE INTERACTING

Use these expressions to describe exactly what you are doing in real-time as you navigate the website or application.

### 1.1. Clicking, Hovering, and Scrolling

- **Clicking:**
  - _"Now, I'm going to click on the 'Submit' button."_
  - _"Let's click here to proceed to the next step."_
  - _"If I click this link, it will redirect us to the billing page."_
- **Hovering:**
  - _"As I hover over this card, you'll see a tooltip showing the details."_
  - _"Hovering over this button changes the state to active."_
- **Scrolling:**
  - _"Let me scroll down to show you the footer section."_
  - _"If we scroll up, the header sticks to the top of the page."_

### 1.2. Filling Forms and Entering Data

- **Entering input:**
  - _"I'll type in a test email address here: `test@example.com`."_
  - _"Let me fill in the required fields with some sample data."_
  - _"I am entering a random value to test the validation."_
- **Selecting options:**
  - _"I'll select 'Credit Card' from this dropdown menu."_
  - _"Let's check this box to accept the terms."_
  - _"I am toggling this switch to enable dark mode."_

### 1.3. Describing UI Changes & Screen States

- **Redirecting & Loading:**
  - _"The page is loading now. It should take just a second."_
  - _"Please bear with me for a moment while the server processes the request."_
  - _"Now the screen has refreshed, and we are on the dashboard."_
- **Modals, Popups, and Alerts:**
  - _"Clicking this trigger opens up a confirmation modal."_
  - _"As you can see, a success notification pops up in the top-right corner."_
  - _"Let's close this modal by clicking the 'X' button or clicking outside."_
- **Pointing out UI elements:**
  - _"If you look at the sidebar on the left, you'll see the navigation menu."_
  - _"Notice the new red badge showing the pending count."_
  - _"At the bottom of this table, we have implemented pagination."_

# 2. GREETING & INTERACTING WITH CLIENTS

### 2.1. Welcoming the Client

- _"Hi [Client Name], thank you for joining our demo today."_
- _"Good morning/afternoon everyone. It's great to have you all here."_
- _"Hi team, today I'll be presenting the latest updates on the web platform."_

### 2.2. Setting the Rules for Questions

- **Allowing interruptions:**
  - _"Please feel free to stop me at any point if you have questions."_
  - _"If you want to discuss anything in detail as we go, just let me know."_
- **Saving questions for the end:**
  - _"I'll run through the demo first, and then we'll open the floor for questions."_
  - _"To keep things on track, I'll take all questions at the end of the presentation."_

### 2.3. Asking the Client Questions & Seeking Feedback (During the Demo)

Keep the client engaged and verify if the implementation matches their expectation.

- **General checks:**
  - _"How does this look to you so far?"_
  - _"Does this layout match what you had in mind?"_
  - _"Is this the expected behavior you wanted for this button?"_
- **Choosing options:**
  - _"Would you prefer this modal to close automatically, or should it stay open?"_
  - _"Should we display this information on the main screen, or hide it behind a hover?"_
- **Verification:**
  - _"Does this flow make sense, or should we simplify it?"_
  - _"Would you like me to walk through this specific step again?"_

---

# 3. OPENING THE DEMO & SETTING THE GOAL

State what you are going to present before sharing your screen.

- _"Today, I'd like to walk you through the new dashboard feature."_
- _"In this sprint, our team focused on optimizing the performance of the checkout flow."_
- _"The goal of today's demo is to showcase the integration with the new payment gateway."_
- _"Before I share my screen, let me give you a quick overview of what we built."_

---

# 4. TRANSITIONS & PROGRESSION

Keep the flow smooth when jumping between features.

- _"Let's move on to the next feature, which is the reporting system."_
- _"Now, if we navigate to the settings tab, we can configure the notifications."_
- _"Moving forward, I'll show you how the admin handles this user request."_
- _"To demonstrate this, I'm going to create a dummy account and run a test transaction."_

---

# 5. BUSINESS VALUE & HIGHLIGHTS

Connect technical work to business outcomes.

- _"One of the key benefits of this update is that it reduces load times by 50%."_
- _"This feature will significantly improve the user onboarding experience."_
- _"By doing this, we eliminate the need for manual data entry, saving time for the admin."_
- _"What's great about this implementation is that it's highly scalable for future features."_

---

# 6. HANDLING DEMO GLITCHES (LIVE BUGS)

Remain calm and professional if something breaks.

- _"It seems we are experiencing a slight technical hiccup. Let me refresh the page."_
- _"This worked perfectly during our staging tests. I'll look into why it is failing here right after the meeting."_
- _"Let's skip this specific flow for now and come back to it if we have time."_
- _"There appears to be a delay with the staging server right now, but you get the general idea."_

---

# 7. WRAP-UP & Q&A

Close the meeting and gather final feedback.

- _"That brings me to the end of my demo. To summarize, we have implemented..."_
- _"Thank you for your time. I'd be happy to take any questions you might have."_

### Handling Difficult Q&A Questions:

- _"That's a great question. From a technical perspective, yes, we can do that."_
- _"I don't have the exact performance numbers right now, but I will find out and get back to you today."_
- _"We haven't considered that specific edge case yet, but it's a valid point. I'll add it to the backlog."_
