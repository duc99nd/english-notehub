# Recipes & Menus

## Reading Skills

When reading recipes and menus, focus on:

- Instructions and cooking steps
- Ingredients and measurements
- Menu categories and descriptions

## Reading 1: Recipe

### Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

**Serves:** 4 people
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cook time:** 20 minutes

**Ingredients:**

- 400g spaghetti
- 200g pancetta or bacon
- 4 egg yolks
- 100g parmesan cheese
- Black pepper
- Salt

**Instructions:**

1. First, bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
2. While the pasta cooks, cut the pancetta into small cubes and fry in a pan until crispy.
3. In a bowl, mix the egg yolks with grated parmesan and plenty of black pepper.
4. When the pasta is ready, drain it and add it to the pan with the pancetta. Remove from heat.
5. Quickly add the egg mixture and toss well. The heat will cook the eggs gently.
6. Serve immediately with extra parmesan on top.

### Notice the language

Read the situation, then listen to the model once for meaning and once for phrasing.
