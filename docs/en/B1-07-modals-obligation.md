# Modals of Obligation & Advice

## Types of Modals

We use modals to express:

- **Obligation** (bắt buộc)
- **Advice** (lời khuyên)
- **Necessity** (sự cần thiết)

> **Pattern**: `Subject + modal + verb`

## Must vs Have to

| Modal       | Use                               | Example                       |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **must**    | Internal obligation (you decide)  | I must finish this today.     |
| **have to** | External obligation (rules, laws) | I have to be at work at 9 AM. |

> "You **must** pass the exam to graduate." (necessary requirement)
> "I **have to** wear a uniform at work." (company rule)

## Should and Ought to

Use for advice and recommendations.

| Modal        | Example                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------ |
| **should**   | You **should** take a break.   |
| **ought to** | You **ought to** see a doctor. |

> "You **should** review your notes before the test."
> "You **ought to** apologize for being late."

## Negative Forms

| Modal                  | Negative      | Meaning                   |
| ---------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------- |
| must not (mustn't)     | mustn't       | prohibited (không được)   |
| don't have to          | don't have to | not necessary (không cần) |
| should not (shouldn't) | shouldn't     | not advisable (không nên) |

> "You **mustn't** smoke in the office." (prohibited)
> "You **don't have to** come early tomorrow." (optional)
> "You **shouldn't** worry about small things." (advice not to)

## Past Forms

| Present | Past                          |
| ------- | ----------------------------- |
| must    | had to                        |
| should  | should have (for past advice) |

> "I **had to** work late yesterday."
> "You **should have** told me earlier."

## Examples in Context

> **At work:**
> "You **must** submit the report by Friday."
> "You **have to** attend the training session."
> "You **should** ask your manager for help."

> **Giving advice:**
> "You **should** take more breaks."
> "You **ought to** learn a new skill."
> "You **shouldn't** skip lunch."
