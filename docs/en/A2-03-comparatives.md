# Comparatives

## How to compare

We use comparatives to show the difference between two things.

> **Pattern**:
>
> - Short adjectives: `adj + -er + than`
> - Long adjectives: `more + adj + than`

## Short adjectives (1-2 syllables)

Add **-er** to the adjective.

| Adjective | Comparative | Example                           |
| --------- | ----------- | --------------------------------- |
| tall      | taller      | He is taller than his brother.    |
| old       | older       | She is older than me.             |
| fast      | faster      | This car is faster than that one. |

## Long adjectives (2+ syllables)

Use **more** before the adjective.

| Adjective   | Comparative      | Example                                |
| ----------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| beautiful   | more beautiful   | She is more beautiful than her sister. |
| difficult   | more difficult   | Math is more difficult than English.   |
| interesting | more interesting | This book is more interesting.         |

## Irregular comparatives

| Adjective | Comparative | Example                             |
| --------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------- |
| good      | better      | This pizza is better than that one. |
| bad       | worse       | Today is worse than yesterday.      |
| far       | farther     | His house is farther than mine.     |
