When reading articles, focus on:

- Main ideas and supporting details
- Author's opinion
- New vocabulary related to health

## Reading: The Rise of Remote Work

### The New Normal

More and more people are working remotely these days. According to recent studies, about 40% of workers now have the option to work from home at least part-time. This shift has brought both advantages and challenges.

### Benefits of Remote Work

Remote work allows employees to save time on commuting and have more flexibility in their schedules. Many people report feeling less stressed without a daily commute. Studies have shown that workers who have a good work-life balance tend to be more productive and satisfied with their jobs.

However, working from home can also have downsides. Some people find it difficult to separate their work life from their personal life. They may end up working longer hours because they feel they should always be available.

### Tips for Better Balance

Experts recommend taking regular breaks throughout the day. You should also set clear boundaries between work and personal time. For example, you might want to stop checking work emails after 6 PM.

Another important aspect of a healthy lifestyle is physical activity. A sedentary lifestyle can lead to health problems in the long run. You should try to exercise for at least 30 minutes every day.
